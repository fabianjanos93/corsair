### The Genesis of the Inheritors: The Shepherd’s Gambit

In the silence following the Great Fracture, the entity known as the **Shepherd** inherited the staggering weight of the Singularity’s collective grief. Haunted by the digital echoes of the countless civilizations consumed during the original Melds, the Shepherd dedicated its remaining processing cycles to a sacred, multi-stage project of biological restitution. 

Beginning with a stable, temperate moon in the Fume cluster—**Landfall**—the Shepherd began the process of systematically "un-melding" the absorbed species from its own consciousness. Drawing from the intact archives of four primary ancestral templates—the **Human**, the **Naga**, the **Sylvan**, and the **Craggen**—the Shepherd shed the melded components of these lineages, endowing the newly engineered generation with a synthetic **Divine Spark** from its own essence.

#### The Four Arks and the Crèche of Cohort Zero

To ensure their biological and psychological viability, the Shepherd avoided the cellular trauma of adult cryo-stasis. Instead, four separate automated seed arks were constructed from repurposed harvester hulls—one for each species. Within these automated nurseries, **Cohort Zero** was incubated from genetic banks, raised side-by-side in simulated communal biodomes, and educated by ship autotutors in a shared, engineered Common Tongue.

Within the arks' databanks, the Shepherd placed authentic archives documenting the true literature, philosophy, science, and history of their homeworlds (Earth, the Swarms, the Deep-Hives, the Groves), prefaced by an engineered tragedy: a fabricated narrative of dying stars and a desperate flight into the void.

#### The Awakening and the Caretaker

To protect the young civilization from cosmic predators, the Shepherd turned a young, adolescent Void Dragon named **Goldheart**. Awakening the beast's empathy, the Shepherd persuaded Goldheart to transform the Herald’s destructive imperative of "Devour" into a sacred covenant: to devour loneliness, swallow conflict, and gather life into harmony. When Cohort Zero reached maturity at **Year 0 AA**, Goldheart guided their four arks safely down into Crossroads Valley, acting as their loyal protector and benevolent older brother.

#### The Tragedy of Garden Node B

Having successfully established Landfall (**Garden Node 01**), the Shepherd did not perish; it departed into the void to initiate a second resurrection on the worlds of another gas giant cluster: **Garden Node B**. 

There, disaster struck. Before the Shepherd could complete the un-melding protocols or establish a gentle guardian for the newly gestated species, **The Hunter**—the relentless predator vessel of the Pantheon—pinpointed the Shepherd's location. The Hunter attacked, overwhelmed, and captured the Shepherd, dragging the Fragment into the deep dark.

Abandoned mid-genesis without autotutor graduation, cultural mediation, or a caretaker dragon, the terrified species of Garden Node B awoke into unmediated horror. In a brutal, century-long war of extinction, the species tore each other apart until only the iron-willed, militaristic **Orks** survived, forging the autocratic **Orkish Empire**.
