Tactical Traits Reference

Operational Doctrines, Reflexes & Specialized Tactical Habits
Acquiring Traits: Fixed flat cost (2 XP to 8 XP). Independent tactical assets with no prerequisite unlock trees or arbitrary gatekeeping challenges.
Character Creation: Starting pool of 6 XP (Hybrids receive 8 XP, Humans receive 10 XP). Spend freely on Skills, Traits, or both.
Downtime Training: Earned XP from completed missions can be invested during day-to-day downtime to permanently acquire new Traits ready for the field.
