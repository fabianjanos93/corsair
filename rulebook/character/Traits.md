# Traits

*You can download and print the [Traits Printable Reference Sheet](Traits_Sheet.html) for play.*

Beyond their fundamental Attributes and learned Skills, operatives across the Sovereign Moons are defined by unique **Traits**. These represent specialized operational doctrines, instinctive tactical reflexes, psychological grit, or customized combat habits honed through years of survival on the fringe. While Attributes measure your raw physical and mental power, and Skills reflect dedicated training with specific Attribute Pairs, Traits grant distinctive mechanical exceptions, alter action economies, enhance team coordination, or open up powerful tactical options.

Traits are designed to add flavor and tactical depth to your operative. They do not merely provide flat numeric bonuses; rather, they grant distinct narrative permissions, modify combat conditions, manipulate the Gambit pool, or allow you to react decisively when circumstances shift.

---

## Acquiring Traits

Operatives acquire Traits by spending **Experience Points (XP)**. Unlike Attributes and Skills—which advance through escalating numeric ratings—each Trait is an independent tactical asset with a fixed XP cost ranging from 2 XP to 8 XP.

There are no arbitrary unlock challenges, prerequisite trees, or artificial gatekeeping requirements for Traits. Any operative who has the required XP may select and purchase any Trait that fits their operational archetype, whether during character creation or throughout ongoing campaign play:

* **At Character Creation:** Operatives receive an initial pool of **6 XP** (with **Humans** receiving **10 XP** and **Human-mixed races**—Drakelings, Elves, and Gnomes—receiving **8 XP**). Operatives may spend this starting XP freely to purchase Skills, Traits, or any combination of both.
* **During Character Advancement:** As operatives complete missions and spend downtime performing training drills, they accumulate additional XP. Operatives can invest this earned XP during day-to-day downtime to acquire new Traits at their listed cost.

Once purchased, a Trait is permanently active and ready for immediate deployment in the field.

## Example: Traits in Action

> ### Tactical Breaching in Station Cargo Bay 4
> 
> Jax and his cell breach a depressurized cargo bay to intercept a squad of corporate security enforcers. Jax holds both the **Duelist** (3 XP) and **Counter Striker** (4 XP) Traits.
> 
> Moving ahead of his team, Jax rushes onto an isolated catwalk to pin down the lead enforcer. Because only Jax and the enforcer occupy that catwalk section, his **Duelist** Trait activates immediately, granting him **+2 Upgrades** on his melee attack test. 
> 
> On the enforcers' turn, the corporate sergeant strikes back with a shock baton, rolling a single Hit (an 8). Jax spends 1 AP to react with a **Contest Roll** using his physical attributes, rolling `[4, 7, 9]`. Scoring a `9`, Jax's Hit eliminates the sergeant's Hit, fully deflecting the blow. Thanks to his **Counter Striker** Trait, Jax does not merely parry the strike—he immediately channels the enforcer's deflected momentum into a punishing counterattack, dealing **9 damage** (equal to his highest rolled contest die) directly to his attacker.

## Melee Combat

These traits enhance an operative's lethality, positioning, and reactive strikes when engaging hostiles in hand-to-hand combat, boarding actions, and close-quarters melee.

### Brawler
* **Cost:** 2 XP

You cannot be outnumbered in melee; hostile creatures gain no Upgrades from Outnumbering when attacking you.

### Combo Master
* **Cost:** 5 XP

You can spend one **Gambit** die to perform an additional melee attack (paying 1 AP as normal), even if the Player Turn would normally end.

### Counter Striker
* **Cost:** 4 XP

If you fully eliminate all Hits when contesting a melee attack, you can deal damage to one attacking creature equal to the highest value you rolled on the **Contest Roll**.

### Duelist
* **Cost:** 3 XP

You gain +2 **Upgrades** on all melee attacks and contests against a creature in your space if you two are the only ones occupying that space.

### Interceptor
* **Cost:** 2 XP

When you successfully contest a creature's movement, you can deal damage to that creature equal to the highest value you rolled on the **Contest Roll**.

### Striker
* **Cost:** 3 XP

Your unarmed attacks deal bonus damage equal to your **Endurance**.

## Ranged Combat & Heavy Ordnance

These traits optimize firearm handling, range calculation, and the devastating deployment of area munitions, grenades, and heavy explosive munitions.

### Marksman
* **Cost:** 4 XP

You can treat your target as being one **Range Band** closer or further for the purpose of determining weapon **Accuracy**.

### Rapid Fire
* **Cost:** 6 XP

You can spend one **Gambit** die to perform an additional ranged attack (paying 1 AP as normal), even if the Player Turn would normally end.

### Gunslinger
* **Cost:** 4 XP

You ignore the 3 Downgrades for firing a ranged weapon while in Close Quarters Combat (CQC).

### Sharpshooter
* **Cost:** 7 XP

You ignore Protection bonuses granted by cover and the 2 Downgrades from firing into Close Quarters Combat (CQC).

### Bombardier
* **Cost:** 3 XP

When you activate a **Crit Effect** on a test made with Ordnance, each spent Hit increases Success Points (damage) by +3 instead of +2.

### Controlled Blast
* **Cost:** 8 XP

When you use an item or an ability that deals damage to all creatures in one or more spaces, you can choose any number of creatures in the area of effect to be unaffected by the attack.

## Defense & Resilience

These traits bolster an operative's physical conditioning, mental fortitude, and ability to protect vulnerable teammates from incoming fire.

### Bodyguard
* **Cost:** 5 XP

When you contest an attack targeting an ally in your space, you gain +2 **Upgrades** on your **Contest Roll**.

### Iron Will
* **Cost:** 4 XP

You gain +2 **Willpower**. This does not increase any other Attributes.

### Stoic
* **Cost:** 5 XP

You gain Mental Protection equal to your **Willpower**.

### Lightning Reflexes
* **Cost:** 8 XP

Once per round, you can contest one attack targeting you without spending an Action Point (0 AP).

### Tough as Nails
* **Cost:** 6 XP

You gain +2 **Endurance**. This does not increase any other Attributes.

### Thick Skin
* **Cost:** 7 XP

You gain Physical Protection equal to your **Endurance** while not wearing armor.

---

## Stealth & Infiltration

These traits empower scouts, saboteurs, and assassins operating unseen within enemy strongholds, maintenance conduits, and sensor shadows.

### Seasoned Stalker
* **Cost:** 5 XP

If the turn ends with you hidden from all non-ally creatures, you can pay one **Gambit** die to take one more action, paying its Action Point cost (1 AP) as normal.

### Sneak Attack
* **Cost:** 3 XP

When you make an attack against a creature that has not detected you yet, the attack deals +3 bonus damage.

---

## Medical & Field Support

These traits improve the efficacy of field trauma medicine, stim administration, and psychological stabilization under fire.

### Healer
* **Cost:** 4 XP

Once per round, when you heal your own or another creature's Physical or Mental Condition, you can heal one additional segment.

---

## Teamwork & Leadership

These traits enhance group tactical coordination, synchronized assaults, and strategic planning when working in tandem with your cell.

### Coordinator
* **Cost:** 3 XP

When you take part in a **Teamwork** Action, every other participant receives 1 **Upgrade**.

### Leader
* **Cost:** 5 XP

Once per round, when you take part in a **Teamwork** Action, a chosen ally can join the action without spending an Action Point (0 AP).

### Planner
* **Cost:** 3 XP

When two or more Delayed Actions are triggered in the same turn, you gain a d10 **Gambit** die.

### Heart of the Team
* **Cost:** 4 XP

Allies can freely transfer any Gambit dice they earn to your **Gambit** pool, and you can transfer **Gambit** dice from your pool to allies whenever they are eligible to spend Gambit.

---

## Social & Negotiation

These traits provide a sharp edge during diplomatic parleys, tense interrogations, high-stakes corporate negotiations, and fast-talking deceptions.

### Closer
* **Cost:** 5 XP

You can spend one **Gambit** die to perform a test that involves communication (paying 1 AP as normal), even if the Player Turn would normally end.

### Impressive
* **Cost:** 2 XP

Your first social interaction test with a creature you have never met before gains +1 **Upgrade**.

---

## Gambit & Momentum

These traits manipulate your cell's shared momentum, allowing you to salvage difficult rolls, step up critical dice, and recover spent resources.

### Called Shot
* **Cost:** 4 XP

When you add a **Gambit** die to a dice pool before rolling, you can re-roll that **Gambit** die once after the roll.

### Double Down
* **Cost:** 5 XP

When you spend two or more **Gambit** dice on a single test, those **Gambit** dice step up to d12s.

### Draw Two
* **Cost:** 4 XP

When you earn a **Gambit** die, if your **Gambit** pool was previously empty, you gain an additional d8 **Gambit** die.
