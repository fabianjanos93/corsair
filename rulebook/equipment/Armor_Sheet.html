Suits, Tactical Properties, Shields & Damage Mitigation

Defense Rules

Protection (Flat Mitigation): Subtract total Protection directly from incoming physical/kinetic damage.

No Min STR: Armor has no STR gates. Any operative may wear any suit within character Load (12+STR×2).

Stacking: Stacks with Shields (+1 to +3) and Cover (+1 to +3). Max 1 worn armor suit.

Formula: Net = Dmg - (Armor + Shield + Cover)

Tactical Properties

Hardened Attacker's Crit bonus reduced by 1 (+1 SP/Hit spent instead of +2).

Blast-Dampened +2 Protection against Area Attacks & Explosives.

Tactical Harness (X) X dedicated Load slots for any equipment (not counted vs Load).

Muffled +1 Upgrade on all tests involving Stealth.

Cumbersome (X) X Downgrades on all tests involving Agility.
